Strategic Takeaways & Observations to Test

1. Contact Fatigue Threshold (Cap Touchpoints at 3)

Observation to Test: Contacting prospects more than 3 times exhibits a 70%+ decline in response conversion while substantially increasing agent costs. Test capping outbound telemarketing queues at 3 touches before cycling the lead into a 90-day cooling period.

2. Student & Retiree Propensity Lift

Observation to Test: Student and retired demographics consistently outperform middle-aged cohorts on fixed term deposit uptake. Test rolling out dedicated high-yield digital term deposit products customized for retiree wealth preservation and student liquid savings.

3. Mobile Channel Prioritization

Observation to Test: Cellular contacts yield noticeably higher conversion than fixed landline numbers. Test pre-dialer phone number validation (HLR lookup) to route cellular prospects to top-tier conversion specialists.
